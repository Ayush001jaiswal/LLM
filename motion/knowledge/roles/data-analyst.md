# Data analyst resumes

## What recruiters look for
Recruiters look for SQL and spreadsheet skills, experience turning raw data into decisions, and tools for dashboards and reporting. They value clear business outcomes: what question was answered and what changed because of the analysis.

## Common keywords
Querying and data: SQL, Excel, Python, pandas, NumPy, R, ETL, data cleaning, data modelling, data warehouse. Visualization and reporting: Power BI, Tableau, Looker, dashboards, KPIs, reporting automation. Analysis: statistics, A/B testing, regression, forecasting, cohort analysis, root cause analysis, stakeholder communication. Use only the terms that match your real experience.

## Strong bullet patterns
Illustrative patterns, with placeholders instead of real numbers:
- "Built [N] Power BI dashboards tracking [KPIs] used by [team] for weekly decisions"
- "Wrote SQL queries to analyse [dataset] and identified [insight], leading to [business action]"
- "Automated a manual report in Python, saving about [X hours] per week"
- "Cleaned and merged data from [N sources], improving reporting accuracy"
- "Presented findings to [stakeholders] and recommended [action]"

## Projects for freshers
Include two or three analysis projects: the question, the dataset, the tools used, the key finding and a link to a notebook or dashboard. Real-world or public datasets with a clear business question stand out more than tutorial exercises.
