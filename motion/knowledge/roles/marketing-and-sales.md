# Marketing and sales resumes

## What recruiters look for
Recruiters look for measurable results tied to revenue, leads or growth, plus the tools and channels used. Numbers matter a lot in these roles: targets, conversion, pipeline, spend and growth.

## Common keywords
Digital marketing: SEO, SEM, Google Ads, Meta Ads, email marketing, content marketing, social media marketing, Google Analytics, GA4, conversion rate optimization, A/B testing, marketing automation, campaign management. Sales: lead generation, prospecting, pipeline management, quota attainment, account management, CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, negotiation, client relationships, B2B, B2C. Use only the terms that match your real experience.

## Strong bullet patterns
Illustrative patterns, with placeholders instead of real numbers:
- "Managed [channel] campaigns with a budget of [amount], generating [N] qualified leads"
- "Grew organic traffic by [X%] through SEO improvements and content updates"
- "Achieved [X%] of quarterly sales quota by [action]"
- "Increased email open rate by [X%] by testing subject lines and segmentation"
- "Maintained relationships with [N] key accounts and improved renewals"

## Showing impact
State the channel, the action and the outcome. If exact figures are confidential, describe the scale in words that are true, such as "for a national retail brand". Never invent numbers.
